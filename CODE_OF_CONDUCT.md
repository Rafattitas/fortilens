# Code of conduct

FortiLens is maintained by volunteers for network engineers and administrators.
Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions is
expected to help keep that work respectful and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and patient, including with people new to FortiGate,
  monitoring or open source.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with evidence and explain why.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project's
  scope, accounting contract or security boundaries.
- Share only sanitized evidence. Never post tokens, passwords, unredacted logs,
  device configurations or other people's personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, such as trolling or repeated off-topic
  posts after a request to stop.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Rafattitas/fortilens/security/advisories/new),
choosing a title that starts with `Conduct:`, or open an issue asking for a
private contact route without repeating the details in public.

The maintainer reviews every report, keeps the reporter's identity private where
possible, and may edit or remove contributions, warn, or block participants who
break this code. Decisions take the situation and any earlier behaviour into
account.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. It is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
