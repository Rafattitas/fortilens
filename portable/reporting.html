        Interpret the evidence

        A successful poll proves that received records were processed. It cannot prove that all traffic was logged and delivered.

        MAC identities are preferred. Missing-MAC identities remain scoped to a session or observation. Shared IPs and changing hostnames never become a verified person.

        Availability excludes unknown intervals. Gaps are neither outages nor recoveries. Route lookup applies to its stated destination and may differ from policy or per-flow SD-WAN selection.
